* `gg_partial_varpro()` labelled categorical partials in the wrong order. Each column of `varPro::partialpro()`'s output belongs to one value of `$xvirtual` (sorted values, or factor levels), but the labels were taken from the order values first appear in the sampled cases, so a 0/1 variable could have "0" and "1" swapped and a multi-level variable had its levels shifted, whenever the two orders differed. On the PBC data this made stage 4 and severe edema look protective. Categorical partials are now labelled by `$xvirtual`. The `$categorical$variable` column now holds `$xvirtual` values; code that filtered it against values taken from `$xorg` should be checked.
